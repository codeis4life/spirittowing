About Spirit Towing

Independent.
Experienced.

Spirit Towing is an independent, owner-operated towing service based in Melbourne’s South East. Founded by Shane, with over 15 years of hands-on experience — a team that genuinely cares about getting you to safety.

We operate a modern fleet equipped for everything from standard car recovery to bogged-vehicle extraction. All operators are fully experienced, licensed, and insured.

  • Fully licensed and insured operators, every job
  • Modern fleet with specialist equipment for all vehicles
  • Trusted network of honest mechanics across Melbourne
  • Competitive, transparent pricing — no surprise fees
  • EFTPOS and major payment methods accepted
